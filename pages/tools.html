Document Operations

Controlled PDF preparation, transformation, and validation. Every operation runs inside your browser, produces a new output document, and leaves the source document unchanged.

Local processing. Documents opened in these workspaces are processed by the document engine running in this browser tab. They are not uploaded to Azure Blob Storage and are not sent to an external document conversion service. After a successful operation, minimal usage metadata is recorded by Prudent Redact and the configured Power Automate trigger is called so plan usage can be counted. PDF bytes and fingerprints are not included in that usage event. Redaction remains a separate, server-side controlled process.
Recent operations
Local operation records. These are stored in this browser only. They are a working history for your own reference, not a server-side audit trail, and they can be cleared by anyone with access to this browser profile. Administrative audit evidence is held separately in Governance.
