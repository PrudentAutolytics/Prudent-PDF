Frequently asked questions
What counts as a "page"?+
Each PDF page counts as one page. A 10-page PDF uses 10 pages from your monthly allowance. Page count is determined by Azure Document Intelligence after processing.
What PII does the system detect?+
The system uses Azure Document Intelligence Custom/Prebuilt models to detect names, email addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers, bank account numbers, addresses, dates of birth, and other sensitive entities depending on your document type.
How long are my files stored?+
All documents are automatically deleted from Azure Blob Storage after 7 days. You can download your redacted PDF within this window. No document data is retained longer than necessary.
Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?+
Yes. Contact us at kabileshvijayakumar@prudentautolytics.com to change your plan. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial?+
Yes — all accounts start with a Free Trial that includes 5 documents, up to 10 MB per file, and up to 50 pages per file. No credit card required to get started.
Where is my data processed and stored?+
All processing happens on Microsoft Azure infrastructure via Prudent Autolytics's Azure subscription. Files are stored in Azure Blob Storage. Document Intelligence runs in Azure's secure environment. No data leaves Azure's infrastructure.
