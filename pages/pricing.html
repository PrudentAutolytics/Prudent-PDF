Frequently asked questions
How is pricing calculated?
Pricing uses real Azure Document Intelligence costs — $1.50 per 1,000 pages for OCR (Read model) and $10 per 1,000 pages for PII detection (Custom/Prebuilt model). We add 20% overhead and apply a 3.5× margin. Per page: ~$0.043.
What happens if I exceed my page limit?
Files that exceed your plan's page or size limit are rejected before processing with a clear error message. Your credit is not consumed. You can upgrade your plan at any time to process larger documents.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All files are stored in private Azure Blob Storage with SAS-based access only — no public URLs. Files are automatically deleted after 7 days. All processing happens within Azure's infrastructure. We never store your document content permanently.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no long-term contracts. Cancel or downgrade at any time. Annual plans are non-refundable after the first 14 days.
Do you offer custom enterprise pricing?
Yes. For organisations with specific requirements — custom Azure deployment, SharePoint integration, volume discounts, or on-premise — contact us and we'll put together a custom proposal within 48 hours.
What Azure services does this use?
Prudent PDF uses Azure Document Intelligence (Read + Prebuilt PII models), Azure Blob Storage, Azure Static Web Apps, Azure Functions, and Power Automate. All services run within a single Azure subscription in your chosen region.
