Frequently asked questions

How is pricing calculated? +
Pricing is based on Azure Document Intelligence costs — $1.50 per 1,000 pages for OCR (Read model) and $10 per 1,000 pages for PII detection (Custom/Prebuilt model). We apply a 20% overhead buffer and 3.5× margin. Per page: ~$0.043.
What happens if I exceed my page limit? +
Files that exceed your plan's page limit will be rejected before processing. You'll see a clear error message and can upgrade your plan to process larger documents.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. All files are stored in private Azure Blob Storage with SAS-based access only. Files are automatically deleted after 7 days. No public access is permitted. Processing happens entirely within Azure's European data centres.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel or downgrade your plan at any time. Annual plans are non-refundable after the first 14 days.
Do you offer custom enterprise pricing? +
Yes. For organisations with specific requirements — custom Azure deployment, SharePoint integration, on-premise options, or volume discounts — please contact us and we'll put together a custom proposal.
