FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Do I need a credit card for the free trial?
No. The 5-document free trial is completely free — sign in with just your work email and you're ready to go. We only ask for payment details when you exceed the free tier.
Where are my documents stored?
All documents are uploaded directly to your Azure Blob Storage container via pre-signed SAS URLs. They never pass through Prudent Autolytics servers. Files are automatically deleted after 7 days unless you configure a longer retention policy.
How accurate is the PII redaction?
Redaction is powered by Azure Document Intelligence (formerly Form Recognizer), which achieves over 95% accuracy on standard identity documents. Confidence scores are provided for each extracted field so you can review low-confidence items manually.
Can I integrate this with my existing Power Automate flows?
Yes. The platform is built entirely on Power Automate HTTP triggers, making it trivial to chain into any existing workflow. Enterprise customers can arrange a full integration review with Prudent Autolytics consultants.
Is the cost calculator accurate?
The calculator uses the same cost model applied to every job. Actual costs may vary slightly based on exact page counts, Azure region pricing, and SendGrid tier. The 20% overhead multiplier covers Prudent Autolytics hosting and support costs.
What document types are supported?
Currently PDF only. Support for DOCX, JPEG, PNG, and TIFF is on the product roadmap. If you need a specific format urgently, contact us — custom extraction prompts can often be configured quickly for enterprise clients.
