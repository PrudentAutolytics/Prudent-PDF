- Document Operations now separates Available now from Coming soon.
- Only core, advanced, and route workflows appear under Available now.
- Controlled workflows that require an unimplemented enterprise engine are locked and disabled under Coming soon.
- Request E-Signatures, Signature Tracking, and similar unimplemented capabilities no longer appear as usable workflows.
- The public access home is redesigned as an enterprise document privacy entry point.
- The login experience uses the same controlled navy enterprise visual system.
- The top right account chip now prioritizes the profile full name.
- Quota refresh synchronizes profile identity into the visible account chip.
- Saving My Profile immediately synchronizes name and profile picture into the top right account chip.
- Existing Power Automate URLs and locked static values are preserved.
- The jobs-submit Power Automate contract remains byte-for-byte unchanged.
