What we do

Everything you need for document redaction

Built for legal, HR, finance, and compliance teams who need to redact PII at scale — without compromising security.

Automatic PII Detection
Azure Document Intelligence scans every page and identifies names, addresses, ID numbers, dates, and more — automatically.
Instant Redaction
Get a fully redacted PDF in seconds. Black-box redactions are burned into the document — no metadata leakage.
Side-by-side Viewer
Compare original and redacted documents side-by-side. Review every redaction before downloading.
Full Job History
Every redaction job is logged with cost breakdown, page count, and timestamp. Export to CSV at any time.
Extracted Fields CSV
Download all detected PII fields as a CSV — names, entities, confidence scores — for audit and compliance records.
Secure Azure Storage
Files are stored in private Azure Blob Storage with SAS-based access. Auto-deleted after 7 days. Zero public access.
